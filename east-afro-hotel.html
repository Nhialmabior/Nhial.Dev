The overview.

East Afro Hotel is a hotel in Juba, South Sudan. Its website presents the hotel online and lets visitors send a reservation enquiry.

The site brings together what a guest needs before arriving: room options, dining, facilities and events, along with clear ways to get in touch. Because rates and availability are confirmed by hotel staff, the booking flow is designed as an enquiry rather than an instant booking.

What’s on the site.

Nine pages, each with a clear purpose, linked by simple navigation.

  • Home
  • About
  • Rooms
  • Dining
  • Facilities
  • Events
  • Gallery
  • Contact
  • Booking

Key features.

Practical features built around how guests actually plan a stay.

  • Reservation enquiry

    Guests choose check-in and check-out dates, number of guests and room preference, then send a request to the hotel team.

  • Indicative availability

    An availability check shows the length of stay, with clear wording that rates and availability are confirmed by staff.

  • Dedicated enquiry routes

    Separate paths for general questions, room reservations and event enquiries.

  • Spam protection

    A hidden form field helps filter automated submissions.

  • Responsive layout

    Navigation and content adapt from large screens to phones.

  • Deployed on Netlify

    Static hosting with fast loading and simple updates.
